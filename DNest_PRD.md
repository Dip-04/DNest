# ❤️ PWA Project — “Our Space”

**Our Space** is a private Progressive Web App designed for **two people in a long-distance relationship**.

It is deliberately **not a messaging or video-calling app**. Couples can continue using their existing communication apps. Our Space focuses on preserving memories, staying emotionally connected, planning future moments, and doing small activities together.

## 1. Main product idea

Only two users belong to one Couple Space.

One person creates an account and generates a private invite code/link. Their partner joins through that invitation. Once paired, both see the same shared relationship space.

The app should feel warm, personal and romantic rather than like a social network.

**Core navigation:**

`Home  |  Moments  |  Together  |  Plans  |  Us`

`🥰 😊 😐 😔 😫`

`❤️ Thinking of You`

`📸 Capture a Moment`

`💌 Leave a Love Note`

`🎲 Find Something to Do`

# 💌 7. Love Notes

Not chat.

Love Notes are intentional little messages that remain part of the relationship space.

Examples:

> “Just wanted to remind you how proud I am of you ❤️”

or:

> “I can't wait to see you next month.”

Notes can have simple themes such as Love, Motivation, Missing You, Good Morning, Good Night and Surprise.

### Scheduled Love Notes

This could be especially useful for couples in different time zones.

Create:

> 💌 Good morning beautiful ❤️

Then choose:

**Deliver tomorrow at 7:30 AM — partner's local time**

The partner gets a notification and opens the note.

---

# ❤️ 8. Thinking of You

A tiny but important feature.

One large heart button:

> **Thinking of You ❤️**

Tap it and the partner receives:

> ❤️ Alex is thinking about you.

No conversation needs to start.

Consider limiting or visually differentiating repeated taps so the interaction continues to feel meaningful.

# 😊 9. Daily Mood

Once per day, each person chooses a mood and can optionally add a short reason.

For example:

> **How are you feeling today?**
>
> 🥰 😊 🙂 😐 😔 😫

Optional:

> “Long day at work, but I'm okay.”

Show a monthly mood calendar for each person.

This is for awareness, not scoring who's “better” at the relationship.

# 💭 10. Daily Couple Question

Every day, provide one question.

Examples:

> What's something you want us to experience together?

> What was your first impression of me?

> What's one memory of us that always makes you laugh?

> Where would you take me if money didn't matter?

> What's something you appreciate about our relationship?

Both partners answer independently.

### Important mechanic

**Partner answers remain hidden until both answer.**

Example:

> Your answer submitted ✓
> Waiting for Maya ❤️

After both answer:

> **Alex:** Japan during cherry blossom season.
>
> **Maya:** Seeing the Northern Lights together.

This creates a reason to return to the app.

# 👩‍❤️‍👨 22. Couple Pairing

### Partner A

Creates account → enters name/birthday/time zone → creates **Our Space** → gets private invitation.

For example:

`OUR-7K92M`

### Partner B

Creates account → chooses **Join Partner** → enters invitation code.

Then:

> ❤️ **You're connected!**
>
> Welcome to your private space.

The code should expire or become unusable after successful pairing.

---

# 🔐 23. Privacy

Privacy is particularly important because users may store intimate personal memories.

Use private-by-default storage, authenticated media URLs, server-side authorization on every couple-owned resource, encryption in transit, secure password/session handling, account export, individual account deletion, couple-space deletion and a clear process for what happens to shared content if the couple disconnects.

A user must **never** be able to access another couple's Moments simply by changing an ID in an API request.

# 🎨 25. UI direction

I'd avoid making everything bright red/pink. It can quickly feel like a Valentine's Day app.

Instead, use a sophisticated warm design: **off-white backgrounds, soft rose accents, warm purple, subtle gradients, large rounded cards, beautiful photography, gentle animations and lots of whitespace.**

Dark mode could use deep navy/charcoal with muted rose accents.

The app should feel like a **private scrapbook + relationship companion**, rather than a dating app.

# 🚀 26. MVP vs later versions

For the **first version**, I would build only: authentication and pairing, Home, Moments + photos, relationship timeline, Daily Question, Thinking of You, Love Notes, next-meetup countdown, wishlist, notifications, and basic settings/privacy.

After the core experience works, **V2** can introduce Memory Map, daily moods, date-night generator, challenges, time capsules, advanced meetup planning, On This Day and richer Memory Books.

Then **V3** can add automatic monthly/yearly recaps, downloadable memory books, home-screen widgets, more personalization and premium themes.

The key product loop should be extremely simple:

**Open app → see partner/relationship → do one meaningful thing → partner discovers/responds to it → relationship history grows.**

That is what can make the PWA worth opening regularly even though it has **no chat and no video calling**.
